# 2026 player signal review

Snapshot date: August 31, 2026

This review covers only QB, RB, WR, and TE players on the 218-player Board. Signals add draft context. They do not change Board rank, sorting, or filtering.

## Definitions

- Injury Risk is a binary editorial watchlist. Inclusion requires at least two corroborating signals, such as a high published model probability, major ongoing recovery, recurring injuries, or repeated missed time.
- Veteran Age uses age on the NFL regular-season opening date, September 9, 2026. Cutoffs are QB 35+, RB 28+, WR 30+, and TE 31+.
- Rookie means a member of the 2026 NFL draft or undrafted free-agent class.
- Team Offense Tier ranks the 32 teams by a two-snapshot average of market-implied points per game. Ranks 1-10 are Good, 11-22 are Mid, and 23-32 are Bad.
- Contingent Upside requires a likely weekly fantasy-starter role after one named teammate becomes unavailable.

## Injury Risk

This is not a prediction that a player will be injured or a recommendation to avoid drafting them. Draft Sharks defines its probability as the chance that a player misses at least two quarters.

1. [RotoWire opening-lines snapshot](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/vegas-implied-totals-best-ball-2026-119601), published August 11, 2026, using Yahoo Sports and DraftKings opening lines for all 272 games.
2. [Sharp Football Analysis consensus feed](https://rmsummerlin.github.io/SFAOdds/data/nfl_implied_odds.json), market update September 1, 2026 at 00:38:40 UTC, using DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Fanatics, and Caesars. [Method documentation](https://www.sharpfootballanalysis.com/fantasy/nfl-implied-team-totals-tool/).

The snapshots are not independent because DraftKings contributes to both. The current feed exposes a consensus rather than separate book values, so this is a two-snapshot average rather than six independently weighted feeds. RotoWire publishes opening PPG to two decimals.
