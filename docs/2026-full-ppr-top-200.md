Draft board for a redraft league with:

- Full PPR scoring
- 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 flex, 1 D/ST, 1 K
- 5-player bench

Round labels assume a **12-team** draft (picks 1–12 = Round 1, etc.).

Updated: July 18, 2026.

Baseline: multi-platform PPR ADP (CBS, ESPN, NFL Fantasy, Yahoo, Sleeper, Underdog), adjusted for this 1-QB roster format. D/ST and kickers are pushed to the final picks.

## Strategy notes

- **Rounds 1–4:** Prioritize elite RB/WR production. Elite TEs (Bowers/McBride) only when clearly above the available WR/RB options.
- **Rounds 5–10:** Build starting depth, then take a difference-making QB or TE at a discount. Do not force QB in 1-QB.
- **Rounds 11–14:** Favor contingent RB upside and receivers with a path to targets. Take D/ST and kicker with the final two selections.
